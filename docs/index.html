Taking a spot

  1. Post GB names or short names and the spots you want filled, highest spot first. Don't post links.
  2. Take spots in order, from the top of the list.
  3. Repost the list without the spot you took, so the next person knows what's left.
  4. Lock your spots after donors pay. If a paid spot gets sniped, the GB owner pays back the donors.
  5. If several people take spots at once, leave and rejoin the chat to see the real order.

How we play Battlegrounds

Every season starts with one question: are we at war, or farming? The answer decides everything else.

Know the season

In a war we're beating a specific rival on victory points. In a farm season we hold our ground and collect rewards. Goods, diamonds and who can watch the map tell us which one we can afford.

Time the capture

Victory points are counted about once an hour. Taking a sector just before the count earns an extra tick and denies it to the other side, so commanders share the exact time at the start of each season.

Fortify and cut off

A fort finished near the count spoils a rival's capture. Traps double their attrition. Holding the right sectors can cut a guild off from the map for hours.

Fight fast

Turn off animations, keep rogues at the top of your army list and level your attrition-reducing GB. Max attrition per player wins wars more than total headcount.

Vindication, our sister guild

Vindication grows newer players and gives experienced ones a lighter schedule when real life gets busy. Members move between the two guilds as their time changes.

Redemption members open their bigger GB spots to Vindication, so smaller players earn more blueprints and medals while they grow.
